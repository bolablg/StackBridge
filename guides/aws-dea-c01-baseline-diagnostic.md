# DEA-C01 Baseline Diagnostic

Use the interactive [StackBridge diagnostic](/data-engineering/gcp-to-aws/diagnostic) before beginning the first formal check-in. It is a baseline signal, not a readiness claim or a substitute for the current AWS exam blueprint.

## How to take it

1. Open the diagnostic from the GCP → AWS Data Engineering path.
2. Answer all 16 questions without notes or search.
3. Record the time taken and your confidence honestly.
4. Note the question numbers where you guessed or changed your answer.
5. Submit the baseline and save the domain breakdown.

## How to use the result

Use the domain scores to choose the next field test:

- **D1 — Ingestion & Transformation:** S3, Glue, Athena, streaming, and orchestration boundaries.
- **D2 — Data Store Management:** Athena versus Redshift, operational stores, migration, partitioning, and performance.
- **D3 — Data Operations & Support:** retries, observability, audit trails, reliability, and cost controls.
- **D4 — Data Security & Governance:** IAM, KMS, Secrets Manager, Lake Formation, and least privilege.

For each missed or uncertain question, write down the service boundary you misunderstood and the evidence that would change your decision. Then connect that remediation to a week on the [study plan](aws-data-engineer-study-plan.md) and record the commitment in the [accountability tracker](aws-data-engineer-accountability.md).

Do not put AWS account IDs, access keys, MFA codes, secret values, or private URLs into the diagnostic notes.
